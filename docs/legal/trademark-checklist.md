# Protecting the ScamShield brand — practical checklist

Not legal advice. Nothing here states that "ScamShield" is available or registered — it is **not
known** whether others already use or own the name. Get a trademark professional to review before
filing, enforcing or licensing.

## What protects what
- **Copyright** protects original expression — the code, documentation, the logo artwork. The code is
  licensed to everyone under Apache-2.0; the artwork and name are not licensed by it.
- **Trademarks** protect brand identifiers — the name "ScamShield" and the shield logo — against
  confusing use. Rights come mainly from **registration** (and, in some countries, from use).
- No licence or policy can technically stop someone from renaming a fork or editing an icon. What
  they provide is a legal basis to act against confusing or misleading use of *your* brand.

## Steps
1. **Clearance search** (before investing more in the name): search for "ScamShield" and close
   variants in
   - India: IP India public search (Trade Marks Registry), classes 9 and 42;
   - US: USPTO trademark search; EU: EUIPO eSearch / TMview; global: WIPO Global Brand Database;
   - app stores, domain names, company registers and the web.
2. **Decide the owner** — you personally or a company; the applicant must be the real owner
   (currently not recorded in this repository).
3. **Classes** typically relevant: 9 (downloadable software / apps), 42 (software as a service,
   security software design). A professional should confirm the specification wording.
4. **File** first where your users are (e.g. India), then consider the Madrid system (WIPO) for
   other countries within the priority period.
5. **Use the marks consistently**: same name and logo in apps, stores, website; "ScamShield™" may
   indicate a claimed mark; use "®" **only after registration** is granted.
6. **Secure the identity**: domain names, GitHub organisation, social handles, app-store developer
   accounts in the owner's name.
7. **Policy and enforcement**: keep `TRADEMARKS.md` current; for misuse start with a polite request;
   app stores and GitHub have trademark complaint processes; escalate only with legal advice.
