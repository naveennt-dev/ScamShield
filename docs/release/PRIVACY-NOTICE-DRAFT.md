**On your device.** Scans are analysed on your device: screen/region images are read with the
operating system's text recognition and are never saved or sent. History (date, result, reasons,
masked text unless you turn text saving off) and your feedback are stored only on your device.

**Only if you choose it.**
- *AI second opinion* (on by default after you agree on first launch; can be turned off): the
  masked text of a scan — numbers, e-mail addresses, UPI IDs and link paths removed — or, for links,
  only the website address and the list of findings, is sent to the AI service for which you
  provided a key. That service's own privacy terms apply; it may log what it receives.
- *Online link checks*: ScamShield contacts the website you asked about (without your cookies or
  accounts) to follow redirects and read the page.
- *Threat-list services* (only if configured): receive the address without query parameters.
- *Share feedback* (off by default): sends your feedback with the masked text to the ScamShield
  service you are connected to.

**Never:** continuous screen monitoring, uploading History or reports automatically, training models
on your scans automatically, selling data.

**Your choices:** turn the AI second opinion, text saving, sharing and launch-at-login on or off in
Settings; delete History in the History window; remove the app and its data folder.
