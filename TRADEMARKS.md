# ScamShield name and logo — trademark policy

The ScamShield **code** is licensed under Apache-2.0 (see `LICENSE`). Apache-2.0 §6 does **not** grant
permission to use the ScamShield name, logo or other brand features. This policy explains what is
allowed. (It describes how we use our brand; it is not a statement that the name is registered.)

**Allowed without asking**
- Saying truthfully that your product works with, or is based on, ScamShield
  ("based on ScamShield", "compatible with ScamShield").
- Unmodified official builds, redistributed together with this policy and the checksums we publish.
- Articles, reviews, tutorials and teaching material that refer to ScamShield.

**Not allowed without written permission**
- Calling a modified version, fork, app, extension, website or service "ScamShield", or using a name
  or logo confusingly similar to it.
- Using the ScamShield name or logo in app-store listings, domain names, social-media accounts or ads
  for anything that is not an official build.
- Suggesting that the ScamShield project endorses, certifies or operates your product or service.

**Forks** must use a different name and logo and must not present themselves as official ScamShield.

**Official builds** are identified by the published SHA-256 checksums (and, once available, the
publisher's code signature). Report misuse or ask for permission via the project's issue tracker.
